Disability Insurance: Protect Your Income

Disability insurance replaces a portion of your income if you're unable to work due to illness or injury. We compare both short-term disability (STD) and long-term disability (LTD) policies from Guardian, MetLife, Unum, and Principal. Key factors include the elimination period (30-180 days), benefit period (2 years to age 65), and own-occupation vs any-occupation definitions. Our advisors help you determine the right coverage amount — typically 60% of gross income — and find group vs individual policy options that fit your budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much life insurance do I need?

A general rule is 10-12 times your annual income, but the right amount depends on your debts, dependents, lifestyle, and financial goals. Our calculator provides personalized recommendations in under 2 minutes.

What's the difference between term and whole life insurance?

Term life provides coverage for a set period (10-30 years) at lower premiums, while whole life offers permanent coverage with a cash value component that grows over time. Term is typically 5-15x cheaper for the same death benefit.

How can I lower my mortgage rate?

Improve your credit score, increase your down payment, buy discount points, compare offers from multiple lenders, and consider a shorter loan term. A 760+ credit score can save you 0.5-1% compared to a 620 score.

Should I choose a Roth or Traditional IRA?

Roth IRA contributions are after-tax but grow and withdraw tax-free — ideal if you expect higher taxes in retirement. Traditional IRA contributions are tax-deductible now but taxed on withdrawal. High earners may benefit from backdoor Roth conversions.

What is an annuity and should I buy one?

An annuity is an insurance product that provides guaranteed income in retirement. Fixed annuities offer predictable returns (4.5-5.8%), while variable and indexed annuities offer growth potential. They're best for retirees who want income certainty, but watch for high fees and surrender charges.
